How we work

Four steps, the same on every project, whether the build is static or WordPress.

  1. 01

    Call

    A short call to work out what the site has to do and who it has to reach.

  2. 02

    Plan

    Structure, copy direction, and a build order. You approve it before anything is designed.

  3. 03

    Build

    Design and build together, in the open, with a live preview link from day one.

  4. 04

    Grow

    Ship it, then layer on SEO, ads, or automation depending on what moves first.

What we hold to

  • You talk to the person building it

    No account manager relaying messages between you and whoever is actually doing the work.

  • Fast is a feature, not a finish

    Speed is designed in from the first decision, not bolted on when the Lighthouse score comes back red.

  • You own everything

    The code, the domain, the hosting account and the ad accounts stay yours. Nothing is held hostage.

  • We say when something will not work

    If a request will cost you money without returning any, you get told before the invoice, not after.
