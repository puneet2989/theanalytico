Privacy policy

This page explains what happens to the information you submit through the contact form on this site.

Last updated 14 September 2026

Who this policy covers

This policy covers theanalytico.com only. The three portfolio sites linked from the Work page (ArdLens, KC Accountants, SodoLT) are separate businesses with their own websites and their own privacy policies; nothing on this page applies to them.

Who is the data controller

TheAnalytico, Rush, Co. Dublin, Ireland, is the data controller for the information described below. You can reach the controller at info@theanalytico.com.

What we collect

The only personal data this site collects is what you type into the contact form:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number, if you choose to give it — this field is optional
  • Your business name, if you choose to give it — this field is optional
  • The service you are enquiring about, selected from a fixed list
  • The message you write

The form also sends a one-time verification token to Cloudflare Turnstile (see below), which is used to confirm you are not an automated script. It is not personal data about you and is not stored alongside your enquiry.

This site does not have user accounts, does not run a newsletter, and does not collect any information from you outside this one form.

Why we process it

We process the information you submit on the lawful basis of legitimate interest: responding to an enquiry you have chosen to send us is a reasonable and expected use of the details you provided for that exact purpose. We do not use your details for marketing, and we do not add you to a mailing list.

Who we share it with

Your enquiry passes through two processors before it reaches us, and no others:

  • Cloudflare — hosts this website and the form's server-side handling (Cloudflare Pages and Pages Functions), and provides Cloudflare Turnstile, the check that confirms a submission is not automated. Cloudflare processes the request and the verification token as part of delivering the site and running that check.
  • Purelymail — hosts the mailbox (info@theanalytico.com) that your enquiry is sent to. Your message is delivered there directly; it is not routed through any other third-party email-sending service.

We do not sell, rent, or otherwise share your information with anyone else.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for two years from the date you submit them. After two years, the email containing your enquiry is deleted from the mailbox and is not retained anywhere else.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation, you have the right to:

  • Ask what information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct it if it is wrong
  • Ask us to delete it
  • Ask us to restrict how we use it, or object to how we use it

To exercise any of these rights, email info@theanalytico.com. We will respond within one month.

Making a complaint

If you are unhappy with how your information has been handled, you can complain to us directly at the address above, or to Ireland's data protection regulator, the Data Protection Commission (opens in a new tab).

Cookies and analytics

This site does not use cookies, analytics, or any tracking script. Nothing on this site follows you between visits or across other websites.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.
